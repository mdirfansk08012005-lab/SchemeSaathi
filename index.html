FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about SchemeSaathi and how it helps users discover government schemes.

What is SchemeSaathi?

SchemeSaathi is an AI-powered government scheme assistant that helps citizens discover government schemes that may be relevant to their personal profile.

How does SchemeSaathi recommend schemes?

SchemeSaathi uses information provided in the user's profile, such as age, occupation, category, income and other available criteria, to calculate a preliminary matching score for government schemes.

What information is used for matching?

Depending on the scheme, the system can consider profile information such as age, gender, category, occupation, disability status and income.

Does SchemeSaathi guarantee my eligibility?

No. The recommendations are preliminary and are intended to help users discover potentially relevant schemes. Final eligibility must always be verified using the applicable official government scheme guidelines.

Can I access the official government portal?

Yes. Scheme details include a link to the relevant official government portal whenever an official link is available.

Can I save my recommendation report?

Yes. After receiving recommendations, users can use the Download / Save Recommendation Report option to generate a printable report that can be saved as a PDF.
